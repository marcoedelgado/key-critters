Mic sensitivity

Use High in a quiet room or with a quiet keyboard, Low if background noise is triggering notes.

Stars

Setting up the keyboard

  • Middle C on a 61-key keyboard is the 3rd C from the left. A C is the white key just left of each pair of black keys. Put the red 🐱 sticker there, then orange, yellow, green, cyan, indigo and pink on the next six white keys.
  • Choose the Piano voice.
  • Keep rhythm, accompaniment and chord mode off — they confuse the listening.
  • Use a medium volume, with the phone on the music stand.
  • Any C counts as C (any octave), so playing the "wrong" C is still right.
  • No mic? The on-screen keys work too — tapping them counts as playing.
